# 1 October 2026 — Native redesign port

## Scope

Port the settled D118–D122 design into actual Clarity under the user’s “ready to port?” instruction. Selected coverage includes **FEAT-shared-ui, FEAT-chat, FEAT-settings**, local command saves across **FEAT-todos, FEAT-notes, FEAT-expenses, FEAT-calendar, FEAT-bookmarks**, and retained workflows/presentation across **FEAT-news, FEAT-mail, FEAT-music, FEAT-agent-board, FEAT-wiki, FEAT-automations**.

External browser spikes remain separate historical evidence.

## Limits and follow-up

The two live Codex scenarios were deliberately gated off; this run does not prove fresh OAuth, live automatic-interpretation quality or same-thread live inference on the new picker. Model output and connected omni-search policy were controlled fixtures. Existing real Gmail/X/Linkding/Navidrome, fresh Spotify OAuth/revocation, listener-confirmed audio, actual macOS positioning, Linux/clean-machine and notarization gaps remain on the [current follow-up](../../discussions/open-questions.md#validation-still-needed). No external messages or provider mutations were sent during this port.

The search result cap and simple matching are deliberate lean limits. Unsupported or ambiguous wording must be corrected in the form; interpretation cannot execute a tool. Session history/preview width reset on renderer reload; existing Chat/expense drafts retain their separate persistence contract. Notes GFM checks are representative, not exhaustive conformance. Minimum-window geometry and keyboard/native controls were exercised; no VoiceOver, touch-device or exhaustive accessibility certification is claimed. The installed process was reopened normally; detailed UI evidence comes from isolated roots, not screenshots of private user data.

The [documentation tests](../../evidence/2026-10-01T142628Z-redesign-port/wiki-tests.log) pass all 13 reader/report cases. Report validation found 10 reports and no errors; the wiki rebuilt to 132 pages and its snapshot/internal links check passed. The [final audit](../../evidence/2026-10-01T142628Z-redesign-port/final-audit.json) records these results and retained-evidence hashes. Those checks establish documentation integrity, not additional product behavior.
