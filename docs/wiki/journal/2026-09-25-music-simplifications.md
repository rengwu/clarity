# 25 September 2026 — Music simplifications

The user clarified that the app is for private use shared with friends and asked to defer the Spotify integration-policy discussion until a public product is considered. Recorded this scope and deferral in [D48](../decisions/index.md#d48-lean-music-scope-and-private-use-25-september-2026), without asserting a policy exemption or continuing to treat it as an active blocker.

Flat playlists are sufficient; nested folders are dropped. Direct local files are explicitly out of scope. For recently played, the user chose the simplest lean approach, accepting the preceding proposal to track collection plays through Clarity locally without reconciling other apps' histories.

The user confirmed switching sources stops current playback and changes the playback context in either direction. Playlists stay on Spotify or Navidrome; Clarity does not own local or hybrid playlists. This clarification does not add a playlist editor to the agreed screen scope.

Updated current topic pages and open questions, retaining earlier journals and marking superseded decision portions. Remaining Music questions concern playback implementation, search/library boundaries, artist behavior, single-search-result continuation, and optional presentation details. No application implementation or account access occurred.
