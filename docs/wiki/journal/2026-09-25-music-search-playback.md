# 25 September 2026 — Music search and playback

The user accepted searching Spotify's full catalogue plus their Navidrome library. They clarified that they do not follow Spotify artists: artist search should cover all of Spotify and Navidrome, rather than the assistant's proposed followed-artists library. Recorded [D49](../decisions/index.md#d49-music-search-and-single-song-playback-25-september-2026).

The user accepted stopping after a single song selected from search. Albums and playlists continue within the selected collection; there is no automatic radio, recommendations, or continuation through search results. Their stated preference is simple behavior with no surprises.

Updated the Music page and resolved these questions in the conversation queue. Artist-detail behavior, optional source styling/search presentation, and playback implementation remain open. The proposed next module discussion is terminal sessions, especially live-process lifetime versus saved traces. No application implementation occurred.
