# 25 September 2026 — Notes and wiki

## User direction

Notes are fully user-maintained and intended for the human, while allowing agents to edit them. Wiki is organized knowledge maintained by agents at the user's request. The example is a user asking for a wiki on a research topic; after creation, it is ideally fully maintained and updated by the agent.

## Changes

Recorded [D26](../decisions/index.md#d26-notes-and-wiki-maintenance-25-september-2026), updated both module pages and the feature map, expanded the recorded chat capability for notes to include editing, and resolved Q03. Added questions for ongoing wiki update triggers and the two module screens.

## Still open

Agent-maintained does not yet specify background activity, schedules, or an automatic research trigger. Those behaviors, wiki browsing and editing controls, and how sources and changes appear remain open. The confirmation/auto modes continue to apply. No storage or framework decision was made.
